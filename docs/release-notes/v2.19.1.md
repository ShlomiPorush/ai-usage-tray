## What's changed

**Codex accounts no longer flip to "Reconnect this account".** Accounts with **Keep session active** ask Codex to refresh their session when the app starts and every 30 minutes. When that refresh failed, the app took it as a sign-out, showed "Reconnect this account" and sent a sign-in notification, although the account was still signed in and came back on the next refresh. The app now reads the account and its usage first and judges the sign-in only by that. The refresh runs afterwards and its result is only written to the log. If the refresh keeps failing for an account, the log says so once, which can be an early sign that the account will need a real sign-in when its current session ends.

**Hovering over the tray icon shows your usage again on right-to-left taskbars.** With a right-to-left display language such as Hebrew, Windows reports the tray icon's position mirrored, so the hover panel with every account's usage never opened. The app now accounts for this on main and secondary taskbars.
