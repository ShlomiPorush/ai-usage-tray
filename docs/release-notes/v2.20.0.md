## What's changed

**Sort the Usage table by any column.** In the Usage window, click a column header in the breakdown table to sort by it, and click it again to reverse the order. Numbers sort by their real value, so 1.20B ranks above 900M. When you sort by API value or share, models without a known price stay at the bottom.

**Pick any range of days.** The new **Custom** button opens a calendar. Click the first day, then the last. A range can be up to 366 days long and can start up to two years back. A range of up to three days shows usage hour by hour. In the day view, click a day to see that day by hour, and the **Back** link returns you to the range, comparison and sort order you had before.

**Compare two periods.** The new **Compare** button lets you pick two periods, A and B, with the same limits. While a comparison is on:

- The total, each provider and the model table show both periods side by side, with the change between them.
- The change always runs from the earlier period to the later one, so a rise shows as a rise. When the periods differ in length, it compares the average per day.
- The chart draws period B as a dashed line, lined up with A hour by hour or day by day, with B's own dates under A's. A range of days that includes today ends at the current hour or day, marked "so far", instead of dropping to zero.
- Notes tell you when one range of days includes today and the other period does not, when the periods differ in length, and when a period starts before the oldest usage on record.

## Known limitations

- When the 24 hours view is compared with a picked period, or a range of up to three days is compared with a period B longer than three days, the chart shows B as its average day, without its own dates or a "so far" mark.
- When period B is longer than A, the chart shows only as much of B as A covers, day by day or hour by hour, so a "so far" mark in the rest of B does not appear. The totals and the table still cover all of B.
- The 24 hours view is not marked "so far", since it always ends now.
- While a comparison is on, the day and hour lists show period A only.
