## What's changed

**Google Antigravity quota.** You can now track a Google Antigravity account next to Claude, Codex, Z.AI and Copilot. Add it in Settings > Accounts > **+ Add account** > **Google Antigravity**. It is off until you add it.

- **Windows:** The Gemini weekly window, and the Gemini 5-hour window on plans that have one, are the main windows. The shared "Claude & GPT" quota is shown as extra rows, together with the plan name.
- **How it reads the quota:** The app reads it from the official Antigravity CLI, `agy`. It starts `agy` in a hidden window for a few seconds at most once per interval you choose: 15 minutes by default, 5 to 240.
- **No stuck processes:** `agy` and everything it starts are closed when the check ends, even if the app crashes. Only one check runs at a time, and the schedule survives a restart of the app or the computer.
- **Privacy:** The app never reads your Antigravity sign-in, never changes the `agy` settings and does not keep or show the account email.
- **Remote view:** When remote view is on, Antigravity appears there like your other accounts, with its own icon. The email is never sent.

**Removed accounts leave the remote view at once.** When you remove or turn off an account, the remote view is updated right away, even if an upload was running at that moment.

**Remote view updates load reliably.** A phone or browser that opened the remote view shortly before an update could keep showing the old page until the next update. The page now loads the new version, and if an update fails to download, the previous version keeps working offline.

**Remote view Usage tab.** The Usage tab now looks like the desktop Usage window. Comparing two periods uses the same picker as the desktop app, and the header fits on one line on phones.

## Known limitations

- **One account:** Only one Antigravity account is supported: the one `agy` is signed in to on your Windows account. Install `agy` and sign in by running it once before you add the account.
- **Relay version:** The Antigravity icon on the remote view needs relay version 1.7.0 or later. Older relays show the account with a letter "A" instead.
- **Sign-in status:** The app cannot tell whether `agy` is signed in. A signed-out `agy` shows "agy returned no quota" instead of a sign-in prompt.
