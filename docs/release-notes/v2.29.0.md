## What's changed

**A larger, easier Settings window.** Settings was small and its text was hard to read. It now opens as a bigger window with the categories in a list on the left and one category on screen at a time, much like Windows Settings. Labels, descriptions, inputs and buttons are larger throughout.

- **One setting per card:** each setting has its name and a short explanation on the left and its switch or choice on the right. Clicking anywhere on the row turns a switch on or off.
- **Updates always in reach:** the version, the update status, **Check for updates** and **Restart app** stay at the bottom of the category list on every page.
- **Theme first:** the **Display** page now starts with **Theme** and **Dark style**. No setting moved to another page.
- **Dialogs to match:** the **Add account**, **Edit account** and **Update available** windows use the same larger text, and **Add** and **Save** stand out as the main button.
- **Keyboard and screen readers:** use Tab to reach the category list and the arrow keys to move between categories. Switches and categories have names that screen readers announce.

## Known limitations

- **Small screens:** on a small or highly scaled screen the window shrinks to fit, and the page and the category list scroll.
- **Same settings, new places on screen:** nothing about how the settings work has changed, but anything you are used to finding by its position may look different.
