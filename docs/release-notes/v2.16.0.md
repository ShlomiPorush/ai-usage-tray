## What's changed

**The floating status panel is now resizable.** Drag any edge or corner to shape the always-on panel to your desktop: provider rows flow into columns as it widens, so four accounts can sit in two rows of two instead of a tall stack. The size you choose is remembered across refreshes, hiding and showing, and restarts. A panel you have not resized keeps sizing itself, including when you add or remove an account, so a new account never hides behind a scrollbar.

**Small touches that make it feel right.** The close button always wins over the resize edge next to it, status text keeps its full content available on hover when a narrow layout trims it, and when the panel is shorter than its content it scrolls without squeezing the text.

Thanks to Product Alliance for contributing this feature.
